I'm Malachi (Kai) Dofat. I'm a liberal arts graduate who got increasingly technical.

I studied English and began my career as an editorial assistant at [Increment](https://increment.com/teams/letter-from-the-editor/), Stripe's quarterly magazine for software development teams.

Over the past four years, I've expanded beyond writing into technical research, data analysis (SQL & Python), and software implementation. Now, I write about technology and build tools and workflows that solve practical problems.

Much of my work has focused on financial software and blockchain infrastructure, but my interests extend to AI, data workflows, and building tools that make research and everyday work easier.

Most recently, I was a grants council member and data analyst for the Metaplex grants program. In this role, I deployed $150k+ to early-stage blockchain startups, developed dashboards to help teams track activity, and produced memos documenting each project's architecture.

Outside of work, I enjoy reading (currently The Devotion of Suspect X by Keigo Higashino), running, and experimenting with my homelab.
