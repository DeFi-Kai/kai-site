I studied English and began my career as an editorial assistant at [Increment](https://increment.com/teams/letter-from-the-editor/), Stripe's quarterly magazine for software development teams.

Over the past four years, my interest in technology has led me to complement writing with data analysis to measure how those systems behave.

Much of my work has focused on financial software and blockchain infrastructure, but my interests extend to AI, data workflows, and building tools that make research and everyday work easier. Most recently, I was a grants council member & data analyst for the Metaplex grants program where I deployed $150k+ to early-stage blockchain startups, developed dashboards to help teams track activity, and produced memos to document each project's architecture.

Outside of work, I enjoy reading (currently *The Devotion of Suspect X* by Keigo Higashino), running, and experimenting with my homelab.
